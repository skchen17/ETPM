# Stage 1.6 G37 interpretation note (post-first-seed, no gate change)

After the first completed formal seed, the curriculum model showed a favorable learned-read effect but a poor *reintroduction* of oracle read after its schedule ended at `p_oracle=0`. The frozen G37 formula uses the curriculum model's own `L_zero-L_oracle` denominator; if that denominator is below its registered .05 floor, G37 must fail, even if learned read performs well. This may measure retention of the oracle-assisted pathway in addition to transfer to learned retrieval. We have **not** changed G37, its denominator, thresholds, checkpoint or seed rule. A separately reported cross-arm fraction using the oracle-trained model's oracle benefit is descriptive only, not a replacement gate. If the pattern replicates, the final report must not mislabel G37 failure as proof of routing failure.
