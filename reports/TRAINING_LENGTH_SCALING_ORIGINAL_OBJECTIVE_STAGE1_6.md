# Original-objective training-length scaling (exploratory)

This is an extension, **not G34–G37**. Eight new B5 seeds were trained on the frozen Stage 1.5 four-family future-event objective for 3000 steps with its selected LR .001, batch 32 and original data schedule. The old historical oracle evaluator was reused on the 128-distractor long-gap slice, tick 4. It does not cover the old 512/2048 long-gap grid. In that old world, past A is also the future class, so its oracle read can approximate the answer itself; this does not test compositional integration the way the new H-scrub world does.
