# Stage 2D.5 — Conditional Interaction Transmission Audit

Research question: when history×candidate-action information exists in persistent-memory reads but has little behavioral effect, where is it lost along read→active-state→fusion, and can a finite interaction-only repair restore behavior?

This is a frozen-mechanism diagnostic. Stage 2D.4 A0/A1 architectures, Stage 2D memory write/consolidation/decay laws, protected evaluator, H recurrence, query/gate topology, dimensions, NULL/SELF_OUTPUT semantics, and observed-only training objective are unchanged. New A1 confirmatory models use the exact Stage 2D.4 A1 train script and hyperparameters. Candidate diagnostics never commit state or advance either clock.

The statistical unit is an independently trained model. The primary readout audit includes 8 matched A0 and 8 A1 Stage 2D.4 endpoints, 16 newly trained A1 endpoints, and 24 independent historical legacy C0 endpoints. Because A1 had fewer than 8 healthy endpoints, the historical legacy cohort supplies the 8 healthy causal-destruction cases; no legacy result is passed off as A1 replication. The primary failed-restoration set consists of the first 8 nonhealthy confirmatory A1 runs in sorted run-ID order. A further 6 original failed A1, all available healthy A1, and F2 controls are secondary checks. The exact names are frozen in `results/stage2d5/processed/selection.json`.

Every activation uses identical 2×2 factorial algebra and records absolute interaction norm and interaction/(state+action main effect) ratio. Adjacent absolute and normalized ratios are both reported. Read-mixing interventions are native, F-only, M-only, equal and norm-matched sum. Residual scales are 0, .25, .5, 1, 1.5 and 2. All are finite, read-only diagnostics. Interaction-only restoration uses the same-architecture healthy mean logit-interaction template, projected into the target model's local activation coordinates through its output-Jacobian adjoint. It tests λ=.25/.5/1 and caps the factorial intervention norm at the healthy native reference (stricter than 1.25×). State-only, action-only, norm-matched random interaction, shuffled-sign and generic-shift controls are retained. Healthy interaction removal/reversal has state/action-main controls.

For the operational gate audit, a rescued checkpoint must meet action-TV≥.10, |IHA|≥.10 and |BS|≥.10 at any allowed λ, and improve both IHA and |BS| by at least .025 over its native endpoint. A necessary-node destruction must reduce both IHA and |BS| by at least half. An earliest node must meet the protocol's ≥6/8 independent-run requirement and outperform every control. A true read→H or fusion bottleneck additionally requires a replicated edge-specific transmission deficit *and* finite rescue at that edge; post-fusion positive-control rescue alone cannot license architecture modification. F2-like cases have max(H-probe, M-probe)≥.75, are nonhealthy, and have |BS|<.10. These implementation thresholds are explicit here; qualitative gate language in the user protocol remains authoritative, and ambiguous effects are conservatively failed.

Architecture modification is authorized only if G100 and at least one of G102/G103 pass. No architecture rescue, expanded replication, dynamics, memory mediation or 10k continuous run may be executed without the specified upstream gates. Memory laws and historical frozen results are not modified under any Stage 2D.5 outcome.
