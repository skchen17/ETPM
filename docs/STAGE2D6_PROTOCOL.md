# Stage 2D.6 — Frozen Fusion Nonlinearity Diagnostic

Question: how do history/state and candidate-action effects become causal behavioral interaction in the existing `Linear → SiLU → Linear` evaluator, and why does training enter that regime inconsistently?

This is a separate diagnostic stage. Stage 2C–2D.5 artifacts are read-only. The persistent F write, F→M transfer, γ=.50, ρF=.97, ρM=.9995, query, read gate, recurrent core, evaluator topology/width/activation, objective and curriculum are frozen. No bilinear term, attention, memory module or new loss is introduced for diagnosis. All branches are ephemeral; parameters, protected evaluator, H/F/M and clocks must remain unchanged.

## Cohorts and labels

- Development: 24 existing Stage 2D.3 C0 legacy endpoints, evaluated through the exact Stage 2D.5 A0 candidate path, including at least 8 healthy and 8 shortcut. Primary eight of each are sorted by run ID. Other development runs remain visible.
- Confirmatory: 24 *new* C0 observed-only runs, 8 new initialization seeds × 3 data streams, with the frozen 1500-step, batch-16, length-4/6/8, evaluator-pretraining recipe and checkpoints at 0/25/50/100/200/300/500/750/1000/1500. No tuning for basin rate. The independent run is the statistical unit.
- Supplementary F1/F2: original and confirmatory A1 endpoints from Stage 2D.4/2D.5, explicitly distinguished from C0. Following the Stage 2D.5 strict F2-like definition, F2 means nonhealthy with max(H,M probe)≥.75 **and** |BS|<.10; F1-like means nonhealthy with max(H,M probe)<.75. High-probe partial cases with |BS|≥.10 are retained as `other_partial`, not silently folded into F1/F2. These are diagnostic phenotypes, not redefinitions of the frozen basin label.
- Frozen healthy definition: action TV≥.10, |IHA|≥.10, |BS|≥.10, CFA>0. Partial/shortcut retain the original classification. No fusion metric enters labels.

All 2×2 factorial conditions use paired history groups and both candidate actions. Sixteen within-run replicates are averaged first. Fusion H projection is itself candidate-conditioned in this implementation. Thus `I_pre = I_Hprojection + I_actionprojection`, with the latter approximately zero, rather than assuming `I_pre=0` from an idealized action-independent state branch. The residual pre-interaction is reported, not hidden. `I_post` is measured after the actual SiLU. SiLU slope and curvature are analytical and cross-checked against autodiff.
Each run records per-neuron 2×2 cell-mean preactivations and SiLU first/second derivatives evaluated at those cell means, alongside aggregate curvature exposure; these do not substitute for the full replicate distribution.

## Frozen primary metrics and rules

- `ΔI_NL = ||I_post||−||I_pre||`, with G110 requiring Δ≥.10 in ≥6/8 healthy in *each* independent cohort.
- Finite behavioral-output response: perturb each post-SiLU unit by ±.02/4 with the interaction sign pattern, measure the four-dimensional *probability-interaction* response, SVD the 4×32 response matrix. Report Q for ranks 1,2,4. The rank-1 Q is primary for G111: healthy exceeds shortcut by ≥.10 in ≥6/8 sorted independent run pairs in each cohort. Unit contribution `C_j` is finite IHA change after removing only unit j's factorial interaction; no gradient is used as the sole evidence.
- Curvature overlap `O=Σ_j |S_j| |A_j| mean_{cells}|SiLU''(z_j)|`, where S is the fusion H-projection state main effect and A is the action-embedding projection action main effect. G112 requires higher O in ≥6/8 sorted pairs and O's run-level AUC to exceed state norm, action norm, mean curvature and fusion norm AUC in both cohorts. Those four single-component comparators were fixed before confirmatory results.
- G113: per-run units are ranked by finite `C_j` and the smallest development k in {1,2,4,8,16} satisfying ≥6/8 is frozen. Removal must lower IHA by ≥.05 and ≥50% of native, and exceed the mean loss of 16 deterministic same-k random removals by ≥.025. Recheck in confirmatory healthy runs. If no k qualifies in development, k=8 is retained as a *diagnostic default*, not a positive development gate.
- The development offset grid is {-1,-.5,-.25,0,.25,.5,1} times the run's native preactivation SD, applied identically across four factorial cells to the selected units. One global signed scale maximizing *mean* development IHA improvement is frozen before confirmatory data. No test-run scale search. Common offset exactly preserves preactivation state/action/interaction contrasts. Controls are random signed same-support/same-norm offset, curvature-lowering offset, 1.5× state projection, 1.5× action projection and a known post-fusion output-aligned positive ceiling. The latter never counts for G114. G114 requires post interaction ≥.025 increase, TV/IHA/|BS|≥.10, IHA and |BS| each ≥.025 improvement, and IHA ≥.025 above each control in ≥6/8 failed in each cohort.
- G115: same-support common shift toward lower curvature at ±3 SD must reduce post interaction ≥25% and IHA and |BS| ≥50%, with IHA at least .025 below a matched random-sign common shift, in ≥6/8 healthy in each cohort. State/action preactivation contrasts are checked, not assumed.
- G116: within-run O half-rise must occur no later than IHA crossing .10 in ≥6/8 healthy in each cohort, exceeding shortcut counts. This is timing evidence, not causal proof.
- G117: F2 must have meaningful state-main effect, poorer Q/O than healthy, and ≥6/8 F2 common-offset behavioral rescues. F2 rescue is a separate gate and cannot be inferred from post-fusion injection.

Training intervention is authorized only if at least two of G114/G115/G116 PASS. If not, G118–G122 are `NOT_RUN_BY_PROTOCOL`; no new fusion initialization, normalization, regularizer, dynamics, mediation or continuous tests are run. If authorized, development is 2 seeds/arm and formal ≥8 new seeds, with later 24-run replication only after G118; any temporary regularizer must be off for ≥80% of training and no latent z, correct-action or memory labels may enter the lifetime model. G120 requires formation/persistence/revision/selectivity, G121 finite F/M mediation, G122 no continuous regression. The F→M handoff remains closed unless G118–G121 all PASS.

`results/stage2d6/processed/frozen_rule.json` records the exact development-selected k and global offset, and whether the new cohort was sealed at freeze. `formal_gates.json` retains all gate calls and numeric evidence. Full per-run traces, interventions, trajectories and negative controls are retained. Neurons and factorial replicas are never treated as independent statistical samples. Failure does not license a fusion or F/M redesign.
