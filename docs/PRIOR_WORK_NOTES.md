# Prior-work overlap notes

ET-RCM overlaps conceptually with recurrent latent computation, fast-weight and
DeltaNet-style associative updates, test-time training, multi-timescale memory,
neural long-term memory, and sleep/replay/consolidation research. The Stage-1
claim is intentionally narrower than novelty or priority over any one family.

The experimental distinction is between **content acquisition** and
**lifetime allocation**. External residual error supplies content. Internal
queries may reallocate already present content between decay timescales, but a
conserving transfer cannot increase the instantaneous total readout.

A literature-grade related-work review is out of scope for this implementation
pass. Stage 2 requires an explicit citation audit before publication claims.
